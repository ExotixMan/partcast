# Google app branding

Use the same app name and identity throughout Google Auth Platform and PartCast:

- App name: **PartCast**
- App homepage: `https://partcast-web.onrender.com/about.html`
- Privacy policy: `https://partcast-web.onrender.com/privacy.html`
- Terms of service: `https://partcast-web.onrender.com/terms.html`
- Public support contact: `npgautoparts@gmail.com`
- Logo download: `https://partcast-web.onrender.com/brand/index.html`

The Google upload is a 120 × 120 PNG under 1 MB. The original vector is `public/brand/partcast-mark.svg`; app icons use the same PC emblem. The legacy Toyota-emblem image has been removed from public assets.

The user-provided Search Console verification tag is in `index.html` and the public homepage. Keep it there after verification. In Search Console, verify the URL-prefix property `https://partcast-web.onrender.com/` with the Google account associated with the OAuth project. Publishing the tag does not click Verify or establish that Google accepted ownership.

After successful ownership verification, save the matching homepage and logo in Google Auth Platform → Branding. Follow Google's displayed 24-hour wait before requesting another branding review. Website deployment does not upload the OAuth logo, submit the review or guarantee Google's approval.
