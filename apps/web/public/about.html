How PartCast uses Google

A Super Admin connects the store’s sending Google account. PartCast sends staff sign-in codes and supplier emails reviewed and submitted by authorized staff. The sending connection can use Gmail API with the Gmail send permission, or Gmail SMTP with a Google App Password.

PartCast does not read the account’s inbox or contacts. Staff sign in with a password and an emailed code; they do not need to connect their own Google accounts.

The optional Google Gemini connection helps answer store questions using the question and relevant store context. See the Privacy Policy for Google access, data use and how to revoke access.
