Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

Your privacy at a glance

PartCast helps NPG Auto Parts manage inventory, sales, customer balances, suppliers and demand planning. We use information to run and protect these services.

Our Gmail connection sends sign-in codes and supplier emails reviewed by authorized staff. It does not read your inbox. We do not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising.

Privacy questions or requests: npgautoparts@gmail.com.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

NPG Auto Parts operates PartCast and is responsible for the personal information it processes through the service. In this policy, “we,” “us” and “our” mean NPG Auto Parts.

This policy applies to PartCast’s public pages, authorized staff accounts, store records and connected services. Customers and suppliers may have information recorded by store staff without having their own PartCast account. Other websites and service providers have their own privacy policies.

2. Information we collect

  • Staff account information: names, email addresses, account identifiers, access roles, account status and authentication/session information. Password authentication is handled through our authentication provider.
  • Store and transaction records: parts, product photos, stock movements, prices, sales, deliveries, references, notes, imported records and staff actions associated with those records.
  • Customer and supplier information: names, contact details when provided, purchases, customer balances or utang, payments, due dates, supplier assignments and reviewed email requests.
  • Google connection information: the sending email address and the OAuth credentials/tokens, Google App Password, or Apps Script deployment URL and signing key supplied by the Super Admin to authorize sending. We also process the recipients, subjects, message content and delivery identifiers needed for authorized emails.
  • Technical and security information: request times, errors, activity records, session identifiers and network information. PartCast’s audit records use a keyed hash of the IP address; hosting providers may separately process connection and request logs.
  • Information you submit for assistance: messages entered into the Store Assistant or sent to our contact email, and relevant store information used to answer a question.

Information comes from you, authorized store staff, imported business records, connected providers and use of the service. Please provide only information needed for the store task and avoid putting sensitive personal information in free-text notes, photos or assistant messages.

3. How and why we use information

We use information to authenticate staff, apply access permissions, maintain inventory and business records, track customer balances and payments, prepare reports and backups, estimate product demand, respond to questions, send authorized emails, investigate errors and protect accounts and store records.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, we process personal information to perform a transaction or agreement, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate business and security interests while respecting your rights, or act on your consent where required. Connecting a Google account requires that account holder’s authorization. This policy does not replace a consent request when separate consent is required.

4. Google account and Gmail access

When Gmail API is selected, the Super Admin authorizes a sending Google account using the Gmail permission https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send and offline access. Offline access allows the server to renew access for authorized sending without asking the account holder to sign in for each message.

When Gmail SMTP is selected, the Super Admin supplies the sending account’s Google App Password. The server uses it to authenticate an encrypted SMTP connection to Gmail. An App Password is an account credential rather than an OAuth permission limited to gmail.send; PartCast uses it only for the email functions described here. Saved App Passwords are encrypted, remain on the server and are not displayed in the settings response.

When Google Apps Script is selected, the account holder authorizes their own deployed script to send mail through Google MailApp. PartCast sends signed HTTPS requests containing the intended recipient and email content to that script. A private signing key authenticates requests, and the script checks the executing sender. The supplied script stores only short-lived request signatures, timestamps and sending states to prevent duplicate submissions; it does not store message bodies or sign-in codes in Script properties. Saved deployment settings and signing keys are encrypted in PartCast. This transport does not read the account’s inbox or contacts.

  • What we send: requested staff sign-in codes and supplier requests reviewed and submitted by authorized staff.
  • What we do not access: PartCast does not read, search or list inbox messages or contacts, or delete existing messages. Google may save sent messages in the sending account.
  • How connection data is used: the sending address and selected connection’s credentials are used to maintain the authorized email connection and send those messages. Credentials stay on the server; saved connection credentials are encrypted in the application database.
  • Sharing: email contents and recipients are transmitted to Google to deliver the message, and recipients receive the addressed message. Necessary connection and message records are processed by our hosting and database providers.

PartCast’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. We do not transfer it for unrelated purposes. Human access to Google user data is limited to circumstances permitted by that policy, such as your affirmative agreement for support, necessary security investigation, or a legal obligation.

For Gmail API, you can revoke PartCast’s access in your Google Account’s third-party connections. For SMTP, revoke the App Password in your Google Account’s App Password settings. For Apps Script, archive the web-app deployment, remove its Google account authorization and remove or replace its signing key. You can also ask our Super Admin, through the contact below, to remove or replace the stored connection and delete associated information where applicable. Revoking the selected connection can interrupt sign-in codes and supplier emails until a working connection is configured. Revocation does not recall emails already delivered or automatically erase store records.

5. Service providers and other disclosures

Information is available to authorized staff according to their roles. We also use providers needed to operate PartCast:

  • Supabase: account authentication, application databases and file storage.
  • Render: website and application-server hosting.
  • Google: Gmail authorization and email delivery, the owner-deployed Apps Script relay when selected, and the optional Gemini service described below.

These providers process information under the applicable service terms and privacy arrangements. Depending on service configuration, information may be stored or processed outside the Philippines. We remain responsible for the disclosures we make and apply safeguards required by applicable law.

We may disclose information when required by law or lawful process, to protect rights and security, or at your direction. We do not sell or rent personal information or share it for third-party advertising.

6. Store Assistant and demand planning

The Store Assistant first uses local guidance and available store records. If the Super Admin enables Gemini and a question needs that service, the question and relevant store context may be sent to Google to generate an answer. Context can include product details, stock, supplier names, sales summaries and demand estimates. Personal information you type into a question may also be included; avoid entering confidential personal details.

Gmail credentials, inbox messages and sign-in codes are not supplied to Gemini through this integration. Gemini processing is subject to Google’s applicable terms and privacy policy. The Super Admin can disable the Gemini connection while keeping local help and record-based answers available.

Product demand estimates use recorded product and sales history for store planning. They support staff decisions and do not automatically decide a person’s access to credit or other legal rights.

7. Browser storage and offline use

PartCast uses browser storage, including local storage, IndexedDB and a service worker, for sign-in sessions, language/display preferences, cached application files, permitted offline records and unsent changes. These are functional features; PartCast does not use advertising cookies or third-party advertising trackers.

Saved offline records and unsent changes can remain on the device until they are cleared. Use a trusted device, protect access to it and sign out when finished. Signing out clears that account’s PartCast offline records from the browser and may remove unsent changes after a warning. Clearing browser data can also remove unsent changes and require a new sign-in. Downloads and copies made outside PartCast need to be managed separately.

8. How long information is kept

We retain information only as reasonably needed for the purposes described here, ongoing business records, account security, dispute resolution and applicable legal or accounting obligations. Different record types may need different retention periods. Deactivating an account or part does not automatically delete past transactions or audit records.

Google connection credentials are retained while needed for configured email connections, subject to revocation and removal. Authentication records and offline access have time limits. Backup retention depends on the store’s settings and provider arrangements; deleted information may remain in protected backups until those copies are rotated or expire. We do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or from a recipient’s mailbox.

9. How we protect information

PartCast uses measures such as HTTPS, password and email-code sign-in, role-based access, restricted administrative controls, encrypted saved integration credentials and activity records. No storage or transmission method eliminates every risk. Report suspected unauthorized access to npgautoparts@gmail.com without sending passwords, sign-in codes or access tokens.

10. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, you may request information about processing, access to your personal information, correction of inaccuracies, and deletion, blocking, objection or restriction where the law provides these rights. Data portability and withdrawal of consent may also apply. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Send your request to npgautoparts@gmail.com. Identify the information or account concerned and the action you want. We may need to verify your identity and authority before releasing or changing records. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and explain any lawful reason we cannot fulfill a request, such as required accounting records or another person’s rights.

You may raise concerns with the Philippine National Privacy Commission or another competent authority. You do not need a PartCast account to make a privacy request.

11. Children’s information

PartCast is intended for authorized business users and is not directed at children. If you believe a child’s information has been collected inappropriately, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or our practices change. We will publish the revised policy here with its effective date and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required by law. A policy update does not itself authorize an unrelated use of previously collected information.
